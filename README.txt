NimbusHR Customer Churn Analysis
Project Overview

NimbusHR is a fictional B2B SaaS company experiencing slower growth in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR). Executive leadership requested a business analysis to identify the drivers of customer churn and recommend strategies to improve customer retention and recurring revenue growth.

This project simulates a real-world end-to-end Data Analytics engagement, following the complete analytics lifecycle from business understanding to executive recommendations.

Business Objective

The objective of this project was to answer the following business questions:

Why has recurring revenue growth slowed?
Which customers are most likely to churn?
Which subscription plans generate the highest recurring revenue?
Which customer segments represent the highest business risk?
Which business actions should be prioritized to improve customer retention?

Key Business Findings
Enterprise subscriptions generate the highest recurring revenue.
Customer retention has a greater long-term financial impact than customer acquisition alone.
A 19% customer churn rate represents a significant opportunity for business improvement.
Customer segmentation provides actionable opportunities for targeted retention initiatives.
Business Recommendations
Prioritize Enterprise customer retention.
Monitor Customer Health Score proactively.
Improve customer onboarding and product adoption.
Optimize acquisition channels based on customer quality.
Continue monitoring customer support performance.
